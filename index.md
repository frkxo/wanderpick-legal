# Wanderpick — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 16, 2026

Wanderpick ("the app") is a mobile app that helps you discover top-rated
places in cities you want to visit. This policy explains what happens with
your data when you use it. The short version: **an account is optional, the
app has no ads and no analytics, and it stores the minimum needed to keep
your saved places in sync.**

## Two ways to use the app

- **As a guest.** Nothing about you leaves your phone. You can save up to
  3 places; they are stored only on the device and disappear if you delete
  the app.
- **With an account.** Your saved places are kept in your account so they
  show up on any phone you sign in on. What that involves is described below.

## What the app sends over the internet

- **City searches.** When you search a city, the app sends the city name and
  the category you picked (for example "restaurants in Paris") to the
  **Google Places API** to fetch places, ratings, reviews and photos. These
  requests are handled by Google under the
  [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app does
  not attach your name, email, or any account identifier to these requests —
  with or without an account.
- **Images.** Place photos and reviewer avatars are loaded from Google's
  servers.
- **Your account** (only if you create one), see next section.

There are no third-party analytics, crash trackers, or advertising SDKs.

## What an account stores

Accounts are hosted by [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) in the
European Union (Frankfurt). When you sign in, the following is stored there:

- **Your email address**, or the identity Apple or Google provides when you
  sign in with them. Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email" is supported.
- **A username** (generated from your email, e.g. `furkan`) and an optional
  **display name**, both changeable on the Account tab.
- **A profile picture**, if you choose to add one. It is resized on your
  phone and stored publicly — anyone with its address can view it, as with
  any profile picture. It is deleted with your account, though a cached copy
  may remain reachable at its old address for up to an hour afterwards.
- **Your saved places**, stored as the place's Google identifier together with
  the city and category you saved it under — never the place's name, rating,
  reviews or photos. Those are fetched again from Google on each device.

Signing in with Apple or Google is subject to their own policies:
[Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) ·
[Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app receives only your
identity and email from them, nothing else from those accounts.

Your saved places are private to you. The app is built so that, in the
future, you may choose to make a city's list public on your profile; until you
do, nothing you save is visible to anyone else.

## What stays on your device

Stored **only locally** on your phone, account or not:

- The full details of your saved places, so "My Trip" works offline
- Your recent city searches
- Your preferences (language, appearance)
- Your sign-in session, if you have an account

## Deleting your data

- **Individual places:** tap the heart again, or use the trash button on
  My Trip.
- **Your whole account:** Account tab → **Delete account**. This permanently
  removes your email, username, name and every saved place from our servers.
  It cannot be undone.
- **Guest data:** uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from
anyone under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated and the
effective date above will change.
